01 — Logo

The note with the check.

A dog-eared card is the "note". The single check stroke is the "right buy". The check leaves the card toward the fold, so the mark reads as decided, not filed away. Use the ember version on dark, the paper version on light. Never rotate, recolor to blue, or add a shadow.

07 — Voice

A note from someone who measured.

Short, specific, kind. We say what fits, who it's for, and what we checked. We never invent a rating, quote a price, or promise a deal.

08 — Rules that never bend

Keep the account safe.

  • Disclosure near the first Amazon link on every page.
  • No Amazon images, prices, stars, review counts or badges unless served through the Creators API. The card slots stay hidden until then.
  • Plain tagged links to amazon.com/dp/ASIN, opened by a real tap. No redirects, no shorteners, no auto-forward.
  • Every card has an original line and a guide link. Polish never replaces content.
  • Generated art never impersonates the exact product. Scene and mood, or an accurate depiction.
  • Tagged links go live only after the site is on the Associates website list with its own tracking ID.
