Breville Bambino, 54 mm espresso

"Compact espresso for a small kitchen: ready in about 3 seconds, and its 54 mm accessories are easy to find."

Buy on Amazon Read the guide
Why we picked it

We chose it for small kitchens: it heats in about 3 seconds and takes 54 mm accessories, which are easy to find.

  • Compact footprint for a tight counter
  • Fast heat-up for weekday mornings
  • 54 mm portafilter: tampers and baskets are easy to find
Size
6.3 in wide, 12 in tall, 13.7 in deep; 10.9 lb
Fits
54 mm baskets and tampers; four baskets, a tamper and a steel milk jug come in the box
Tank
47 oz, lifts out at the back

Skip it if you want a built-in grinder. Measure your cabinet clearance first.

Before you buy

  • Counter and cabinet clearance: it is 12 in tall and 13.7 in deep, and the 47 oz tank lifts out at the back, so leave a gap behind it or plan to reach over.
  • Accessory size: 54 mm. Buy tampers, baskets and funnels sold for Breville 54 mm, not 'universal' 58 mm ones.
  • Grind plan: no built-in grinder. It ships with pressurized and non-pressurized baskets, so you can start on pre-ground and add an espresso grinder later.

Fits: A small kitchen that wants cafe-style milk drinks from a machine that is hot in about 3 seconds.

Not for: Anyone who wants a grinder built in, or who already owns 58 mm tools.

Goes with

  • Baratza Encore ESP burr grinder: the grinder with a real espresso range
  • Escali Primo digital kitchen scale: weigh the beans and the shot
  • Chemex 8-cup pour-over coffeemaker: a pot on the slow mornings
