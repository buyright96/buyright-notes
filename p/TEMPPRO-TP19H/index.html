Why we picked it

We chose it for the baker or roaster who guesses and wants to stop.

  • 1-second reading, accurate to 0.9 F, per TempPro
  • Wakes when you pick it up, sleeps after 90 seconds; the display rotates so it reads from either hand
  • Rinse-clean: IP65 water resistant, per TempPro

Range: -58 to 572 F; probe 4 in, stainless; runs on one AAA battery for up to 3000 hours, per TempPro

Warranty: 3 years after registration, per TempPro

Skip it if you want a leave-in probe that stays in the oven; this one is for quick checks, not for monitoring a roast from the couch.

Before you buy

  • Quick-check only: it does not stay in the oven; for a roast you monitor from the couch you want a leave-in probe.
  • Power: one AAA battery, up to 3000 hours per TempPro; a battery is listed as included on the current page.
  • Name: ThermoPro became TempPro in 2026; the TP19H model number is the same.

Fits: A home cook who wants a one-second, rinse-clean answer to whether the bread, roast or oil is at temperature.

Not for: Anyone who wants a leave-in probe with an alarm.
