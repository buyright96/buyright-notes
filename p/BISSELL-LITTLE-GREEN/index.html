Clean home

BISSELL Little Green Mini, spot cleaner

"A small spot cleaner for spills and pet accidents: it sprays, scrubs and lifts the mess from couches, rugs and car seats."

Buy on Amazon Read the guide
Why we picked it

We chose it because it handles the messes that actually happen: coffee on the couch, a pet accident on the rug, crumbs in car seats.

  • Sprays, scrubs and suctions in one pass
  • Stain tool and self-rinsing hose tool included
  • Small enough to keep in a closet
Size
13.5 in wide, 12.1 in tall, 10 in deep; 7 lb
Tank
16 oz
Includes
4 in tough stain tool, HydroRinse self-cleaning hose tool, 8 oz of Little Green formula
Power
corded

Skip it if you need to clean whole rooms of carpet. Empty and rinse the dirty-water tank after each use.

Before you buy

  • It is a spot cleaner: a 16 oz tank for couch cushions, rug stains, stairs and car seats, not whole-room carpet.
  • Corded: check that an outlet reaches the spot you clean most, or plan an extension cord.
  • Formula: 8 oz comes in the box; the manual names what else is safe to use in the tank.

Fits: Homes with pets, kids or a fabric couch, where the same small messes happen every week.

Not for: Anyone who needs to deep-clean whole rooms; rent or buy a full-size carpet cleaner for that.
