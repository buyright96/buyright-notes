What portafilter size does my espresso machine use?

Espresso accessories are sold by basket diameter, and the sizes are not interchangeable. Look yours up before buying a tamper, basket, funnel or portafilter.

Look up your machine

Type your espresso machine to see its portafilter size and which accessories fit. Sizes are starting points; confirm against your exact model number before buying.

The full model number is on the base or back of the machine, not just the brand.

Diameter is a starting point, not a fit guarantee: basket depth, ridges, locking tabs and machine generation still differ. Sizes checked against manufacturer pages in September 2026; this tool has no affiliate links.

If your machine is not listed

  • Look for the number stamped on the portafilter or in the manual's specifications page.
  • Measure the inside of the basket rim to rim; 51, 54, 57 and 58 mm are the common sizes.
  • Then search accessories by your exact model number, not by "universal".
