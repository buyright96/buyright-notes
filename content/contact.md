# Contact BuyRight Notes

Questions, corrections, and feedback are welcome. Write to us about:

- a factual correction or a broken link;
- a fit or compatibility detail that needs clarifying;
- an accessibility problem on the site; or
- a privacy question.

Include the product name or page address and enough detail to find the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information, tax information, or other sensitive personal information.

BuyRight Notes cannot give individual repair, electrical, medical, legal, financial, or child-safety advice. Product availability, prices, specifications, and policies change; verify important details with the manufacturer or retailer before buying.
