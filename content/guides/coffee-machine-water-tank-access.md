# Coffee Machine Water-Tank Access: Measure This Before You Buy

The measurement that matters is the refill path: where the water tank comes out, and whether you can reach it, lift it and put it back without moving the machine. The full planner, with all five measurements, is in the **[Home espresso setup guide](../home-espresso-setup/#will-an-espresso-machine-fit-my-kitchen)**; the short version is here.

## Find the tank first

A machine can fit on paper and still be a chore every morning if you cannot refill it without dragging it out. Tanks fill three ways, and each needs a different kind of room:

- **Top-fill.** Works under cabinets only with room to open the lid. Measure the height under the cabinets with the lid open.
- **Rear-fill.** Needs a real gap behind the machine, or every refill becomes a chore. Measure the depth with a gap behind for the cord and the tank.
- **Removable side or front tank.** The most forgiving. It only needs that side left open.

Body dimensions tell you whether the machine fits; the refill path tells you whether the station works. Confirm the exact model number and read its current dimensions before ordering; same-brand machines differ by generation.

## Three compact machines, sorted by the tank
