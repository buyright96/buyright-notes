# Coffee Machine Water-Tank Access: Measure This Before You Buy

A coffee machine can fit your counter and still be frustrating every morning if you cannot refill the water tank without dragging it out.


Start by finding the tank location. A top-fill tank needs open height above the machine. A rear-fill tank needs space behind it. A removable tank needs enough room to lift or slide it free. Your exact manual is the final word.


Measure four things before you shop: the counter depth, the height below cabinets, the gap behind the machine, and the safe path for the power cord. Then add working room for cups, a portafilter, and any steam wand.


Top-access machines usually work well beneath cabinets only when you have enough clearance to open the lid and remove the tank. Rear-access machines need a real gap; pushing them tight to the backsplash may turn a simple refill into a daily chore. A removable tank can be convenient, but only if nearby shelves and cabinets do not block its path.


Do not assume a machine can be moved daily without consequence. Water, hot surfaces, cords, and a crowded counter make repeated repositioning a poor setup.


Bottom line: machine dimensions tell you its body size. Tank access tells you whether the station will actually work. Measure the refill path before checkout, then verify it against the manufacturer manual for the exact model.
