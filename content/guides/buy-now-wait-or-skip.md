# Should You Buy an Espresso Machine Now? A Buy, Wait, or Skip Checklist

Before checkout, write the problem the product must solve and the one constraint that could make it unusable. Buy now when the need is immediate and fit and returns are clear. Wait when a key measurement or compatibility detail is missing. Skip when the product adds work without solving the original problem.
