# A Simple Coffee Machine Cleaning Schedule That Starts With the Manual

Good coffee equipment lasts longer when cleaning is part of the normal routine, not a rescue project. The safest schedule begins with the manufacturer manual because procedures and approved products differ by machine.


After each use, remove grounds or capsules, empty the drip tray when needed, rinse the parts your manual allows, and wipe milk residue from the steam wand immediately. Milk residue hardens quickly, so this is the task that is hardest to postpone.


Weekly, clean the tray, wipe the exterior, check the water tank, and inspect the areas around the group head or capsule compartment for old grounds and moisture. Let removable pieces dry fully before storage when the manual calls for it.


Monthly tasks depend on the machine. Some models need a cleaning cycle, filter check, or deeper rinse. Descaling is not a universal monthly job: water hardness, machine design, and the manufacturer’s schedule matter. Never guess chemical dosage or use a cleaning product the manufacturer does not approve.


A useful setup includes a small tray or drawer for the machine-approved cleaning supplies, a clean cloth, and a place for drying parts. The right organization makes the routine easier without blocking water-tank access or ventilation.


Bottom line: follow your exact model manual first. A simple repeatable routine is safer and more useful than an aggressive generic cleaning plan.
