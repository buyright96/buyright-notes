# Whole Bean or Pre-Ground Coffee for Espresso? Start Here

One rule decides it: **whole bean** if you own an espresso-capable grinder, **pre-ground** if you want the fewest steps. The full answer is in the **[Home espresso setup guide](../home-espresso-setup/#do-i-need-a-grinder)** under "Do I need a grinder?"; the short version is here.

## Whole bean, if you can grind for espresso

Espresso needs a much finer, narrower grind range than drip coffee, and grinding your own buys you the fix: a shot that runs too fast or too slow is corrected with a grind change, and a drip grinder cannot make it. Grind close to brewing and keep the bag sealed away from heat and light.

The 54 mm dosing cup matches the Bambino, a 58 mm adapter is in the box, and a 51 mm machine works with the cup held under the chute. Quiet enough for an early house. ## Pre-ground, if you want the fewest steps

Pre-ground is not a beginner mistake. Buy coffee sold for espresso or a fine grind, and accept that you cannot make small adjustments when a shot tastes sour or bitter. A pressurized basket makes a fair shot from it, and both machines we list ship with one.

- **Breville Bambino, 54 mm.** Pressurized and non-pressurized baskets in the box: start on pre-ground, switch baskets when the grinder arrives. The catch: accessories are Breville 54 mm, not the common 58 mm. [Our note](../../p/BAMBINO-54/)
- **CASABREWS CM5418, 51 mm.** One-cup and two-cup pressurized baskets in the box, so pre-ground works from day one. The catch: 51 mm is the odd accessory size; search by model name, never "universal". [Our note](../../p/CASABREWS-51/)

## The quick check

- Have an adjustable espresso grinder? Whole bean.
- Want the fewest steps? Pre-ground, sold for espresso or a fine grind.
- Machine ships with a pressurized basket? Pre-ground works on day one.
- Moving to a non-pressurized basket? Whole bean, grinder first.

## Either way, the first bag

Buy a small bag you can finish while it still tastes good, pick tasting notes you already like, and change one thing at a time when you dial in: grind, dose, yield or time.
