## How we check a deal

A sale badge is easy to make and easy to trust. These are the four things we look at before a deal goes on this page, in the order that saves the most time.

- **Did it hold for a day?** Some "limited time" prices flip back within hours; a real event price sits there. We recheck after 24 hours and drop anything that moved.
- **Who is selling it?** "Ships from and sold by Amazon.com" or the brand's own store. Third-party listings during a sale are where the wrong-model and no-warranty stories come from.
- **What does it usually sell for?** The crossed-out number is the list price, which may be a price nobody paid this year. Compare against what it sold for last month; a price-history tool shows that in one chart.
- **Would we buy it at full price?** If the answer is no, a discount does not change it. Every item on this page is one we already like or would add to the shelf.

## Before you buy anything on sale

The checks above tell you whether the deal is real. These tell you whether the product is right for you, which matters more.

- **Measure first.** Counter depth, cabinet height, the spot under the desk. A sale price does not make a 17-inch machine fit under a 16-inch cabinet.
- **Read what is in the box.** Brush heads, filters, pods, a cover: the deal bundle sometimes has fewer than the standard one, and sometimes more.
- **Check the delivery date, not the badge.** Prime shipping is fast, but event-week stock runs out; the date shown at checkout is the one that counts.
- **Start small if you are not sure.** A cheap item you already know you need makes the first order easy; the bigger item can go in the same cart once you trust the seller.
- **Know the return window.** Amazon posts the current return policy on every listing; read it before you buy anything you cannot test in a store.

## Questions people ask

### When are Prime Big Deal Days 2026?
October 6 and 7, 2026. Early deals start appearing in the week before, marked as early Prime deals on the listing.

### Do I need Prime to get the prices?
Yes for the Prime-exclusive deals, which is most of them. Some listings show a lower price to everyone; the listing says when a deal is Prime exclusive.

### Is a Prime Big Deal Days price the lowest of the year?
Not always. Some items go lower on Black Friday, and Amazon devices are often close to the same price at both. If you can wait, compare against last November in a price-history tool.

### Should I wait for Black Friday instead?
If you need the item before Thanksgiving, buy it now if the deal passes the checks above. If it is a gift you can hold, waiting costs little and may save more.
