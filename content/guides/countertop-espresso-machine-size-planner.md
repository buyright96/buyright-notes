# Espresso Machine Counter Space: Measure Before You Buy

The planner now lives inside the **[Home espresso setup guide](../home-espresso-setup/#will-an-espresso-machine-fit-my-kitchen)**. The short version is here so you can act on it in a minute: the five measurements, the compact machines that fit most counters, and the direct links.

## The five measurements

- **Usable counter width.** The clear stretch where the machine will live, with room beside it for a cup and, if you grind fresh, the grinder.
- **Depth.** Backsplash to the safe front edge, with a gap behind for the cord and for rear-fill tanks.
- **Height under the cabinets.** Top-fill tanks and pod lids need open air above the machine.
- **The refill path.** Top, rear, or a tank that slides out the side: can you reach it, lift it and put it back without moving the machine?
- **The cord and the outlet.** A safe path to power, away from the sink and the drip tray.

Confirm the exact model number and read its current dimensions before ordering; same-brand machines differ by generation. Not sure which machine to measure for? The **[size finder](../home-espresso-setup/#fits-my-kitchen)** asks three questions and points at one.

## Compact machines that fit most counters
