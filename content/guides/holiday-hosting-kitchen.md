## Appetizers without the oven

The oven holds the main dish; the air fryer handles the trays. A 6-quart square basket fits two trays of appetizers or a whole chicken, and it reheats and crisps leftovers the next day.

- **Size:** 6 qt is the hosting size. For one or two people a 4 qt takes less counter space; that is the reason to skip the big one.
- **Range:** the Cosori TurboBlaze runs 90 to 450°F with 5 fan speeds, so it also dehydrates and reheats. Cosori rates it under 53 dB on the highest fan, quiet enough to run while people talk.
- **Care:** a PFAS-free ceramic nonstick basket and crisper plate; both come out to wash.
- Counter check: 14.4 x 11.8 x 11.9 in and 13.2 lb, with 1725 W, so give it its own outlet and some space behind for the vent.

## The cheese board that arrives complete

A cheese board is the appetizer that needs no cooking, and the set matters more than the cheese: knives, bowls and labels are what people forget.

- The SMIRLY bamboo set is one box: a 15.7 x 13.3 in board with 3 slide-out drawers for the knives and serving pieces, 4 bowls, a fruit tray, 2 slate labels and chalk markers.
- Bamboo resists water and scratches, per SMIRLY; hand wash only.
- It is about 10 lb and needs cupboard space, so skip it if you host rarely. SMIRLY is an Amazon marketplace brand, not a store name you will know; the set is the point, not the label.

Assemble it an hour ahead, cover with a damp cloth, and pull it out when the first guest arrives.

## Party drinks in one pitcher

Frozen drinks for a crowd need power and a big pitcher, not a personal blender.

- The Ninja Professional has a 1000-watt motor with stacked blades for ice and frozen fruit, and a 72 oz pitcher that holds 64 oz of liquid: about eight drinks a batch.
- Buttons for low, medium, high and pulse. No heating mode, so it is not for hot soups.
- It is tall and loud, and the blades are sharp when you wash the pitcher. Skip it if you want a quiet blender or single-serve cups.

## Dessert made the day before

A pint of ice cream made the day before is the easiest dessert to host with, and the "make your own pint" station is the party trick.

- The Ninja CREAMi freezes a pint base for 24 hours, then spins it into ice cream in minutes. 7 one-touch programs: ice cream, gelato, sorbet, milkshake, smoothie bowl, lite ice cream, mix-in. Re-spin for a softer texture; Mix-In folds in chips, nuts or cookies.
- Two 16 oz pints come in the box, and the pints, lids and paddle are top-rack dishwasher safe.
- The catch is the 24-hour freeze: skip it if you want ice cream tonight, or if freezer space for pints is tight.

## What to skip

- A second air fryer or a bigger one: 6 qt already covers a party tray; two machines fight for outlets.
- Specialty cheese knives beyond the set: the four in the box cover soft, hard and spreadable.
- A blender with a heating mode just for one soup a year; the stovetop does that.
