# 51 vs. 54 vs. 58 mm Espresso Accessories: Stop Buying the Wrong Size

Before you buy any espresso accessory, find the full model number on your machine, then match the size: the millimeter number is the basket diameter, and fit can still depend on basket depth, tabs and machine generation. The full version is the **[accessory size section](../home-espresso-setup/#which-accessory-size-51-54-or-58-mm)** of the Home espresso setup guide; the short version is here.

## The four sizes

- **51 mm:** compact consumer machines, including the [CASABREWS CM5418](../../p/CASABREWS-51/) and the De'Longhi Dedica line. Most kits are 54 or 58 mm and most "universal" tampers are 58 mm, so search by the model name CM5418.
- **54 mm:** most Breville and Sage home machines, including the [Bambino](../../p/BAMBINO-54/) and the [Barista Express](../../p/BARISTA-EXPRESS-54/). Buy parts sold for Breville 54 mm; a 58 mm tamper will not seat.
- **57 mm:** Lelit's own group on the Anna PL41; 58 mm parts do not fit.
- **58 mm:** most prosumer and commercial-style groups, and the most stocked tamper size. Some baskets are 58.5 mm.

Not sure which size yours is? The **[Portafilter Size Finder](../home-espresso-setup/#already-have-a-machine-in-mind)** lists it by brand and model, with what to buy and skip; in the guide it sits under [Already have a machine in mind?](../home-espresso-setup/#already-have-a-machine-in-mind)

## Check before you buy

Shop by exact model first and size second. A listing that says "universal" but does not name your machine is missing information, not reassurance. Before checkout, confirm the full model number (same-brand machines differ by generation), the exact part, and that the accessory maker names your model.

## Which parts are risky to guess

Lowest risk to highest:

- **Tamper:** a poor match leaves an untamped ring.
- **Dosing funnel:** must match the diameter and the mounting.
- **Puck screen:** needs headspace in the basket.
- **Replacement basket:** depth and ridges matter.
- **Full portafilter:** locking tabs and group fit must match too.
