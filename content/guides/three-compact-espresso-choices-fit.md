# Three Compact Espresso Choices: Fit Before You Buy


A compact espresso setup only works if the machine, grinder, and surrounding space fit your real counter. These recommendations use documented specifications, not hands-on testing.


Breville Bambino: a fit-based option for a 54 mm accessory path. Measure the counter before buying.

[Check current Breville Bambino offer on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B1JPPG2L?tag=buyrightnotes-evergreen-20)


Baratza Encore ESP: its documented 54 mm dosing cup and 58 mm adapter help with many setups, but confirm yours first.

[Check current Baratza Encore ESP offer on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BW272XCV?tag=buyrightnotes-evergreen-20)


CASABREWS CM5418: a lower-entry 51 mm setup. Do not buy 54 mm or 58 mm accessories for it.

[Check current CASABREWS CM5418 offer on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09X3WGJ3R?tag=buyrightnotes-evergreen-20)


Pick the machine first, then verify portafilter diameter, water-tank access, and grinder workflow.
