# Privacy policy

*Last updated September 23, 2026.*

BuyRight Notes runs this shop and the guides at [buyrightnotes.blogspot.com](https://buyrightnotes.blogspot.com/). This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit and how third-party services may use it.

## Information collected automatically

This site is served by a hosting provider that may automatically receive information such as your browser and device type, approximate location, IP address, referring page, and pages viewed. The guides are hosted on Blogger, a Google service, which receives the same kinds of information. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

BuyRight Notes uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general site use, such as page views, traffic sources, device categories, and taps on outbound links. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies to collect and process data. We use these reports to improve the shop and the guides; we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

How Google uses information from sites that use its services: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

Google Analytics privacy information and opt-out options: [support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245)

## Affiliate links and Amazon

Pages contain Amazon affiliate links. If you select one, you leave BuyRight Notes and visit Amazon. Amazon may collect information about your visit and purchases under Amazon's own privacy notice. BuyRight Notes may receive aggregated reporting such as clicks, ordered items, and qualifying commission; Amazon does not give us your payment information.

BuyRight Notes participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate, BuyRight Notes earns from qualifying purchases.

Amazon Privacy Notice: [amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ)

## Cookies and your choices

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by our host, Google Analytics, Amazon, Pinterest, or other services reached through links on this site. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although doing so may affect how some services work. Where consent is required, the applicable platform or consent tool presents the choice.

## Information you choose to send

If you contact BuyRight Notes, the information you provide is used to answer your message and keep necessary business records. Do not send payment information, passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive personal information.

## Sharing and retention

BuyRight Notes does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host, measure, secure, or operate the site. Those providers apply their own retention practices. Direct correspondence is kept only as reasonably needed to answer it, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

## Children

BuyRight Notes is general consumer buying guidance and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit personal information for a child through this site.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's tools or practices change. The date above is revised when a material update is published.
